# MIYAR — Competition Demo

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** A reference is not proof by itself; MIYAR makes the relationship between a claim and its evidence visible before publication.
- **Identity:** Match the real MIYAR interface: warm ivory, deep evergreen, muted sage, and restrained ochre. Use IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic copy. Use the supplied logo unchanged.
- **Hero:** Real browser captures of the current MIYAR application, including actual results from the public verification endpoint.
- **Motion:** Close editorial crops that connect claim to reference; deliberate camera pushes and drawn focus brackets over the real UI; short mask/match transitions carried by the green claim/status line.
- **Arc:** Open with the problem, move into the product, follow one real Quran verification, then compare only real result states and resolve on the MIYAR mark.
- **Payoff:** The final app screen contracts into the supplied logo and exact tagline; this replaces the evidence-card compositions used through the middle.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 78.89 s (2,367 frames), H.264 MP4. Arabic RTL.
- **Sound:** Supplied Arabic voiceover is the unchanged master track. No music or added sound effects.
- **Truth rule:** The four test runs already returned: Quran claim `موثق`; invalid hadith number `تعارض في الإحالة`; consensus claim `الدليل غير كافٍ`; no run returned `يحتاج مراجعة بشرية`. Use only their actual application screens and results. The human-review label may appear only with its real zero count.
